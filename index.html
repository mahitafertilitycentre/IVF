IVF Treatment in Hyderabad

In vitro fertilization (IVF) is an assisted reproductive treatment in which eggs and sperm are combined in a laboratory. A suitable embryo may subsequently be transferred into the uterus. Treatment is individualized according to age, fertility investigations, ovarian reserve, sperm parameters and medical history.

Steps in IVF Treatment

  1. Initial consultation and fertility assessment.
  2. Ultrasound and appropriate investigations.
  3. Ovarian stimulation and follicular monitoring.
  4. Egg retrieval and sperm preparation.
  5. Fertilization using IVF or ICSI when indicated.
  6. Embryo development and assessment.
  7. Embryo transfer and pregnancy follow-up.

IUI Treatment

Intrauterine insemination (IUI) involves placing prepared sperm into the uterus around the time of ovulation. It may be suitable for selected patients following evaluation of fertility factors and tubal status.

Fertility Evaluation and Reproductive Care

Fertility assessment may include menstrual and pregnancy history, ultrasound, ovarian reserve testing when indicated, tubal assessment and semen analysis. Depending on the findings, treatment options may include ovulation induction, IUI, IVF, ICSI or selected laparoscopic procedures.

IVF does not guarantee pregnancy. The benefits, risks and expected outcomes should be discussed with the fertility specialist before treatment.
